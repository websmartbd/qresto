HOW IT WORKS

Effortless Dine-in with Table QR

When you dine with us, everything is designed to be smooth and convenient right from your table.

01

Take a Seat & Scan

Find your table in the dining room. Each table has an individual QR stand. Simply open your phone camera and scan to access your table docket instantly.

No app download or sign-up needed
02

Select & Customise

Browse dishes with complete photos, prices, portions, and add-ons. Add custom kitchen instructions (less spicy, no onions, extra sauce) and submit directly to the chef.

Real-time kitchen order ticket (KOT)
03

Enjoy Fresh Table Service

Watch your order status update from "Cooking" to "Served". Need assistance? Tap "Call Waiter" from your phone screen anytime during your meal.

Pay at table or counter when finished
